Hybrid is architecturally stronger than PSK injection because it provides per-session PQC forward secrecy and tighter binding between authentication and key agreement, at the cost of larger messages and a more invasive implementation. PSK injection is a practical deployment shortcut that achieves most of the confidentiality benefit without touching WireGuard's internals.
